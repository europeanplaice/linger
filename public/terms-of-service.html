2. Service Description

Linger is a personal diary web app. It uses Google sign-in and Google Drive to store diary entries as plain-text files in your Google Drive. Cloudflare Pages and Workers provide the app's hosting, authentication, and backend requests to Google Drive. Your entry contents pass through the backend while it handles those requests.

Linger also offers an optional S3 backup. If you configure and enable it, Linger copies existing entries to the AWS S3 bucket you specify and mirrors subsequent saves and deletions. Google Drive remains the primary copy. Backup processing requires the app to remain open and authorized. You may need to authorize Google again if its permission expires or is revoked. See the Privacy Policy for details about data processing and service providers.

S3 backup covers diary entries, not milestones or other app settings. It is not end-to-end encrypted: entry contents pass through Linger's backend, and diary files remain readable in Google Drive and the configured S3 bucket.
