Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. Overview

Linger is a personal diary web app. Your Google Drive is the primary store for diary entries. Linger's Cloudflare backend handles sign-in and requests to Google Drive. It processes diary text while carrying out those requests, but does not use Cloudflare databases as a diary store. If you enable the optional S3 backup, Linger also sends diary files to the S3 bucket you configure in your own AWS account.

This policy describes information Linger processes, the service providers involved, and the choices available to you. Google, Cloudflare, AWS, and other providers may also process information under their own privacy terms.

2. Information Linger Processes

  • Google account information: your email address and a stable Google account identifier, used to sign you in and keep sessions associated with the right account.
  • Diary and app files: entry text and dates, milestone labels and dates, and (if configured) S3 backup settings and sync status. These files are stored in the Linger folder in your Google Drive, normally linger_diary/. Google Drive may also keep file revisions.
  • Authentication and sync information: OAuth tokens, session identifiers, Drive file identifiers, and, when S3 backup is configured, the AWS role and bucket settings and entry sync progress or error status.
  • Browser data: preferences, holiday-calendar selection, milestones, cached diary entries, offline drafts, and pending changes that help the app work across reloads and during network interruptions.
  • Technical information: website and API request information, service error diagnostics, and the aggregate traffic and performance metrics described below.

3. Google Sign-In and Google Drive

Linger requests the Google OAuth scopes openid, email, and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. The first two identify your Google account. The Drive scope lets Linger create and manage the specific files you use with the app; it does not grant general access to your entire Drive. Linger uses this access to read, create, update, search, restore, and move its diary files to Google Drive Trash.

Google provides sign-in and stores your diary files, milestones, and any S3 configuration files. Your entries are plain-text files. Google controls its own storage, revision history, and deletion practices. See Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Cloudflare Hosting, Sessions, and Sync Metadata

Linger runs on Cloudflare Pages and Workers. When you read or save an entry, its contents pass through Cloudflare's network so the backend can make the authorized Google Drive request. The backend does not use its authentication database or sync index to store diary text.

Cloudflare D1 stores session metadata, including your session ID, Google email and account identifier, expiry, and Drive file identifiers. OAuth access, refresh, and identity tokens and AWS temporary credentials are not persisted in session records. Short-lived Google tokens are held in browser memory and sent with authorized requests; the backend uses them transiently. Reloading or closing the page discards those tokens, and expiry requires authorization again. Session records expire 30 days after creation or renewal; sign-out invalidates the session. The browser's opaque linger_session cookie identifies the metadata record and cannot authorize Drive access by itself. On HTTPS it is HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Strict. Sign-in also uses a five-minute OAuth state record and a temporary linger_oauth_state cookie to bind the callback to your browser. On HTTPS that cookie is HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax. The state record does not contain Google access, refresh, or identity tokens.

Cloudflare's coordination services store operational metadata such as Drive file IDs, entry dates and versions, S3 sync status, and backfill progress so writes and backups can be coordinated and retried. This metadata does not include diary entry text. Cloudflare may also process request logs, error diagnostics, and workflow state under the retention rules for the services and account plan in use. Cloudflare documents Workers Logs retention of up to seven days and Workflow instance-state retention of up to 30 days, depending on plan and settings. See Workers Logs retention, Workflow retention limits, and Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Earlier versions stored credentials server-side. Removing live session records does not revoke Google authorization or erase historical backups. Previously stored credentials must be revoked separately; users can remove Linger's access in their Google Account permissions and authorize again. This policy does not claim that deleting a database row removes every historical copy.

5. Access by the Linger Host Administrator

Here, "host administrator" means the operator who controls Linger's Cloudflare project and deployed code. Linger has no admin screen for browsing users' diary entries, and its database and sync index are not used to store diary text. However, Linger does not use end-to-end encryption: diary text and request-scoped OAuth tokens pass through its backend.

Linger's source code is public on GitHub so users can inspect how the current implementation handles data. The production GitHub Actions workflow deploys from main after its validation jobs and associates the deployment with the triggering commit hash. This makes the normal deployment traceable to public source; it does not remove the host administrator's technical access described below or prevent an administrator with Cloudflare deployment access from changing the running code.

Information the host administrator may access, or cause Linger to access

  • Cloudflare session metadata, including your Google email and account identifier. These records do not retain OAuth credentials and cannot by themselves authorize requests to Drive.
  • Diary entries and milestones while processing your authorized requests. The backend receives tokens transiently for these operations. An administrator who changes deployed code could capture those tokens or contents, so we cannot guarantee inaccessibility to an administrator with deployment access.
  • If you enable S3 backup, the configured bucket's contents within the permissions of your AWS IAM role during authorized operations. Linger uses temporary credentials in request memory to mirror and restore entries.
  • Operational records available in Cloudflare, such as sync status, entry dates and versions, backfill progress, and service logs or error details.

Information the current backend does not receive or grant general access to

  • Your Google account password. Google handles the sign-in and does not send the password to Linger.
  • Google Drive files outside the files available to Linger through the drive.file authorization.
  • Browser storage as a whole. An unsaved offline draft that has not been sent to Linger remains on your device; it is sent only when the app syncs or submits it.
  • Local exports, clipboard contents, or other device data that you do not submit or share through the app.

The last list describes what the current backend receives in normal operation; it is not a technical barrier against someone who controls the deployed code. A host administrator could change the app to collect information from browsers when they load it. Do not treat Linger as an end-to-end encrypted diary service.

6. Optional AWS S3 Backup

S3 backup is off unless you configure and enable it in Settings. You provide an AWS IAM role, bucket, and region. Linger stores those settings in your Google Drive and uses your Google sign-in identity to request temporary AWS credentials from AWS Security Token Service. When backup is enabled, Linger backfills existing Drive entries and mirrors later saves and deletions to the configured bucket. Diary text and entry dates are sent to that bucket; AWS also receives the identity token used to assume the role and the requests needed to write the objects. Linger does not ask you for long-term AWS access keys. Backup work advances while the app is open and authorized; credentials are not saved in durable background jobs.

The bucket is in your AWS account and its permissions, region, versioning, lifecycle, and other retention settings are controlled by you and AWS. Turning backup off stops future syncing but does not remove objects already in the bucket. A delete request may leave older object versions or retained copies according to your bucket configuration. See AWS's Privacy Notice.

7. Browser Storage, Exports, and Sharing

Linger uses browser localStorage and IndexedDB for preferences and for local copies of entries, cached content, unsaved drafts, and pending offline saves or deletions. Milestones and a few account and interface hints may also be stored there. In local or offline use, an entry may remain in browser storage until it can be synced. This browser data is not separately encrypted by Linger and may be accessible to someone who can use your browser profile or device. Linger clears diary caches and pending changes when you sign out or switch accounts; some display preferences can remain. You can also remove local data by clearing this site's data in your browser.

If you export a ZIP or portable HTML file, the resulting file is downloaded to a location chosen by your browser. If you use the entry-sharing control, the selected text is passed to your device's share sheet or clipboard. What happens after that depends on the destination app or service you choose.

8. Other Providers and Analytics

If you select a holiday calendar, Linger sends the selected country and year through its backend to Nager.Date to retrieve public holiday data. Linger caches those results in Cloudflare KV for up to 90 days. Diary entries are not included in that request. The app also requests fonts from Google Fonts; those browser requests may disclose connection and browser information to Google. See Google Fonts' FAQ.

The website includes Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate page-visit and performance measurements, such as page views, referrer, country, browser/device category, and Core Web Vitals. Cloudflare says Web Analytics does not track individual users across websites and does not collect visitors' personal data. It does not use an analytics cookie. Cloudflare keeps unsampled beacon data for seven days, then aggregates it; analytics are available for up to six months. See Cloudflare Web Analytics and its retention details.

Linger does not use advertising or behavioral ad tracking.

9. Retention and Deleting Your Data

Your diary files and milestones remain in Google Drive until you delete them there. Deleting an entry in Linger moves its Drive file to Google Drive Trash; permanently remove it from Trash if you want it deleted from Drive. Google controls the retention of revisions and trashed files. You can also remove the Linger diary folder from Drive.

When S3 backup is enabled, Linger sends a delete request for the corresponding current object when you delete an entry. You are responsible for removing any remaining S3 object versions and for deleting backups when you no longer want them. Turning off backup alone does not delete them.

Sign out to invalidate your Linger session and clear locally cached entries. Account-scoped unsynced drafts remain in this browser so they can be restored after the same account is verified again; clear this site's browser data to remove them. To revoke Linger's Google authorization, visit Google Account → Apps & services. Revoking access does not delete files already stored in Drive or S3. For questions about personal information held by Linger's services, contact us below.

10. Security

The app uses HTTPS for communication between your browser, Cloudflare, Google, and AWS. OAuth tokens are kept in browser memory and used transiently by the backend. No method of online storage or transmission is completely secure, and the security of your Google account, device, browser, and optional AWS account is also important.
